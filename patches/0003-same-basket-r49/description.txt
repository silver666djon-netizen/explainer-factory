Does your ride really cost more when your battery is low? Case file #002 follows Larry (fictional) through one day: a viral battery rumour, a TV that got $100 dearer in a parking lot, and a real 2025 investigation in which hundreds of shoppers paid different prices for the same groceries at the same store, at the same moment.
