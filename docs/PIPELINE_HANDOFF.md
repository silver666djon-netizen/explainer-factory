## 6. Standard cycle for a fix list
1. Map every item to its beat in `gen_script.py`; fix in the script, or in the engine when it is a general rule
   (then also add the rule to PRODUCTION_RULES.md).
2. `python episodes/<slug>/gen_script.py`, then check locally on the previous release's `timings.json`:
   `python tools/qc/mark_audit.py episodes/<slug> <timings.json> --shorts` (must be 0 exceptions) and render the
   affected frames from the plan (build_plan + `shot.frame(t)`) to look at them before spending a CI render.
3. Commit (end message with the Co-Authored-By / Claude-Session lines), push, dispatch render `send=no`.
4. Download the release. On the NEW `timings.json`: `mark_audit.py --shorts` (0 exceptions),
   `EP=episodes/<slug> python tools/qc/handcheck.py timings.json long` and `... shorts` (0 issues),
   `python tools/qc/audio_gate.py long.mp4 short_s*.mp4` (all PASS, ≤ −1.0 dBTP, ≈ −14 LUFS). The build also
   fails by itself on true peak > −1.0 dBTP and logs `WARNING max-still` for any still > 8 s.
5. Extract frames at every fixed timecode (ffmpeg `-ss`), make one contact sheet, look at it, confirm each item.
6. Report: per item what changed + timecode; audit/handcheck/gate numbers; anything deviating from his
   wording and why. Then Notify. Then the next stage he asked for.

## 7. Engine features you will need
- Marks are in fractions of the ORIGINAL picture. `write(text, x, y, w, h, on="word", angle, color
  red|ink|paper, fnt marker|typewriter|body|headline, delay, dur)`; `at=0.0` without `on` = already there.
  Typewriter font without hand = typed letter by letter.
- `plate(x, y, w, h)` = cream plate already on the picture; put a `write()` on it (readable over busy art).
- `closeup(on, center, zoom, marks) | {"back": s, "back_marks": [...]}` — push-in from a word, then pull back
  to the full frame for the last `s` seconds; `back_marks` are written only after the pull-back (delay from its
  start). Beat `hold=s` adds picture time after the line.
- Visual `push={"center": [x, y], "zoom": z}` = whole shot on a detail.
- Doc card: `push_on="word"`, `push_zoom` (default 0.32), `push_ease` (seconds or "rest").
- Beat `hero=True` / `ledger_hide=True` hides Larry's ledger; ledger safe zone = top-right 18 % × 22 %.
- Engine keeps written labels inside the visible frame, shrinks circles/crosses to the visible part only when
  their `target` still fits, and centres Shorts' square window on target marks. Every circle/underline/cross
  needs a `target` box.

## 8. Known pitfalls (already hit once)
- The native AAC encoder overshoots (up to +5 dB) on high-frequency transients → AAC cutoff 18 kHz, limiter
  0.72, gate measures the encoded file and remasters audio-only up to 3× before failing (r61's first render
  failed the gate at +1.6 dBTP — that is how this was found).
- Hand must leave the frame before the cut (HAND_OUT 0.32 s) → give the last hand action time (`hold`).
- Square Shorts window crops wide pictures; the audit catches marks written outside the frame.
- Strike-through must cross every line; stamps on paper are pressed by the stamp hand, never a pen hand.
