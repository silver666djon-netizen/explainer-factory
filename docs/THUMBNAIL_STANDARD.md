# CoinSense thumbnail standard (ChatGPT image prompt)

Workflow: 1) send ChatGPT the episode script with the "concepts" request → 10 ideas;
2) pick one; 3) send the standard below + the chosen idea; 4) put the result in
episodes/<slug>/thumbnail.png (the build uses it as is).

```
THUMBNAIL STANDARD — CoinSense (apply to the concept below)

FORMAT: YouTube thumbnail, 16:9, 1920x1080. Photorealistic cinematic still, like a frame from a crime-drama film: dramatic low-key lighting, warm tungsten highlights, deep dark shadows, shallow depth of field, subtle film grain. Detective mood (desk lamp, magnifying glass, case-file papers, dark wood) is welcome in the background.

COMPOSITION: one clear main subject, 50–60% of the frame, centre or right of centre, strong silhouette, sharp focus. Maximum three objects in sharp focus; everything else soft and dark. Readable at the size of a matchbox on a phone screen.

TEXT: 2–4 words only, ALL CAPS, along the top of the frame, one or two lines, text height about one fifth of the frame. Font: heavy condensed sans-serif (Anton / Bebas Neue / Impact style). First word(s) white #FFFFFF, the key word(s) bright yellow #FFE600. Thin dark shadow for contrast. Spell every word exactly as given. The text must NOT repeat the video title — it adds the missing emotion or question.

SIGNATURE MARK: exactly one hand-drawn red marker circle or arrow (#E53935), slightly imperfect, around the single most important detail (a number, a price, an object).

NUMBERS: if a number is used, it is large, bold, black or white, and fully legible.

PERSON (optional): if a person appears, use the series hero "Larry": a middle-aged man with a small moustache, red fedora, yellow-green checked jacket, white shirt, black tie — expressive face (shock, doubt, temptation). Otherwise no faces.

COLOURS: dark background (near-black, deep green or dark wood); accents only red and yellow; warm light on the main subject.

SAFE ZONES: keep the bottom-right corner (20% of width × 20% of height) dark and empty — YouTube puts the video length there. Keep 5% margins from all edges for text.

DO NOT: logos, brand or store names, real people, watermarks, extra captions, small unreadable text (props like receipts may show a few short words), clutter.

CONCEPT FOR THIS EPISODE:
[paste the chosen idea here, plus the exact thumbnail text in quotes]
```

Concepts request (put above the script):

```
Here is the full script of my YouTube video (channel CoinSense — psychology of money, detective "case file" format). Suggest 10 different thumbnail concepts for it. For each: the scene in one or two sentences, the main object, the emotion, and 2–4 words of thumbnail text (ALL CAPS) that does not repeat the title but adds curiosity. Make them varied: objects, numbers, a person's reaction, a contrast, a mystery. Number them 1–10.
```
